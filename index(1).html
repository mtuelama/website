Who we are

MTÜ Elama is a non-profit organisation based in Estonia. We design and run international projects funded by Erasmus+, bringing young people and youth workers together to learn from each other.

What we do

We organise training courses and mobility projects for youth workers (Key Action 1) and develop cooperation projects with partner organisations across Europe (Key Action 2).

We are open to new partnerships. If you work with young people and want to build a project together, write to us.

Projects

The AI-augmented Youth Worker: From Guardian to Guide

A training course for youth workers in Estonia, held with partner organisations from Italy, Turkey and Bulgaria. It looks at how to use generative AI responsibly in youth work.

Against the Current: Travelling Differently

A youth exchange held in France, where we take part as a partner organisation. It looks at sustainable tourism, digitalisation and techniques to spread blue citizenship.
